# P10 — Units & Measurements 📘 Priority 3

> **Exam Importance:** SI units, dimensional analysis, and measurement instruments are tested at moderate frequency. Knowing fundamental vs derived units, instruments for specific measurements, and dimension formulas is sufficient for this chapter.

---

## 1. SI System (International System of Units)

### 1.1 Fundamental Units

| Quantity | SI Unit | Symbol |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Length | Metre | m |
| Mass | Kilogram | kg |
| Time | Second | s |
| Electric current | Ampere | A |
| Temperature | Kelvin | K |
| Amount of substance | Mole | mol |
| Luminous intensity | Candela | cd |

> There are **7 fundamental (base) units** in SI.

### 1.2 Supplementary Units

| Quantity | Unit | Symbol |
|---------|------|--------|
| Plane angle | Radian | rad |
| Solid angle | Steradian | sr |

## 2. Important Derived Units

| Quantity | Derived Unit | In Base Units | Symbol |
|---------|------------|--------------|--------|
| Force | Newton | kg·m/s² | N |
| Pressure | Pascal | N/m² = kg/m·s² | Pa |
| Energy/Work | Joule | kg·m²/s² | J |
| Power | Watt | J/s = kg·m²/s³ | W |
| Frequency | Hertz | s⁻¹ | Hz |
| Electric charge | Coulomb | A·s | C |
| Voltage | Volt | J/A·s | V |
| Resistance | Ohm | V/A | Ω |
| Capacitance | Farad | C/V | F |
| Magnetic flux | Weber | V·s | Wb |
| Magnetic field | Tesla | Wb/m² | T |
| Inductance | Henry | V·s/A | H |
| Luminous flux | Lumen | cd·sr | lm |
| Illuminance | Lux | lm/m² | lx |

## 4. CGS vs SI System

| Quantity | CGS Unit | SI Unit | Conversion |
|---------|---------|---------|-----------|
| Length | cm | m | 1 m = 100 cm |
| Mass | gram (g) | kilogram (kg) | 1 kg = 1000 g |
| Force | Dyne | Newton | 1 N = 10⁵ dyne |
| Energy | Erg | Joule | 1 J = 10⁷ erg |
| Pressure | dyne/cm² | Pascal | 1 Pa = 10 dyne/cm² |

## 6. Dimensional Analysis

**Dimension formula** expresses a physical quantity in terms of base dimensions: M (mass), L (length), T (time), A (current), θ (temperature), N (amount), J (luminous intensity).

| Physical Quantity | Dimensional Formula |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Velocity | [M⁰LT⁻¹] |
| Acceleration | [M⁰LT⁻²] |
| Force | [MLT⁻²] |
| Work/Energy | [ML²T⁻²] |
| Power | [ML²T⁻³] |
| Momentum | [MLT⁻¹] |
| Pressure | [ML⁻¹T⁻²] |
| Density | [ML⁻³] |
| Frequency | [M⁰L⁰T⁻¹] |
| Surface tension | [MT⁻²] |
| Viscosity | [ML⁻¹T⁻¹] |
| Angle | Dimensionless [M⁰L⁰T⁰] |
