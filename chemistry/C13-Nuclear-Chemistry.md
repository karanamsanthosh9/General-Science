## 1. Transmutation

**Definition:** Conversion of one element to another by nuclear reactions (alpha, beta decay, or bombardment).

**Natural transmutation:**
```
²³⁸U → ²³⁴Th + ⁴He (alpha decay — U changes to Th)
₉₂       ₉₀       ₂
```

**Artificial transmutation (Rutherford, 1919 — first artificial):**
```
¹⁴N + ⁴He → ¹⁷O + ¹H
₇       ₂      ₈      ₁
(Nitrogen bombarded with alpha particles → Oxygen + proton)
```

## 2. Nuclear Reactions vs Chemical Reactions

| Property | Chemical Reaction | Nuclear Reaction |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Involves | Electrons (outer shell) | Nucleus |
| Elements change? | No | Yes (transmutation) |
| Energy change | Small (kJ/mol) | Very large (MJ or GJ/mol) |
| Affected by conditions (T, P, catalyst) | Yes | No |
| Isotopes behave | Same | Differently |
| Mass | Conserved approximately | Slight mass deficit → E = mc² |

---

## 3. Mass-Energy Equivalence

```
E = mc²     (Einstein's equation)
where:
E = energy (Joules)
m = mass (kg)
c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s

Mass defect → Binding energy (energy holding nucleus together)
```

---

## 4. Nuclear Stability

**Belt of stability:** Graph of neutrons vs protons for stable isotopes.
- Light elements: N/P ratio ≈ 1:1
- Heavy elements: Need more neutrons (N/P > 1)
- Above Z = 83 (Bismuth): All isotopes are radioactive

**Magic numbers:** Nuclei with 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, or 126 protons or neutrons are especially stable (complete shells).

## 5. Radioactive Decay Series

| Series | Starting isotope | End product |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| Uranium series | ²³⁸U (₉₂) | ²⁰⁶Pb (₈₂) |
| Thorium series | ²³²Th (₉₀) | ²⁰⁸Pb (₈₂) |
| Actinium series | ²³⁵U (₉₂) | ²⁰⁷Pb (₈₂) |
| Neptunium series | ²³⁷Np (₉₃) | ²⁰⁹Bi (₈₃) |

## 6. Nuclear Fission Chain Reaction (Chemistry Perspective)

```
¹n + ²³⁵U → [²³⁶U]* → ¹⁴¹Ba + ⁹²Kr + 3¹n + 200 MeV
₀     ₉₂       ₉₂      ₅₆      ₃₆   ₀

Each neutron produced can cause another fission → chain reaction
```
