# C14 — Colloidal Chemistry & Solutions 📖 Priority 4

> **Exam Importance:** Low-moderate frequency. Know true solution vs colloid vs suspension, Tyndall effect, types of colloids, and properties. SSC exams occasionally include these.

---

## 1. Types of Mixtures

| Type | Particle Size | Visibility | Filtered? | Example |
|------|-------------|------------|-----------|---------|
| True solution | < 1 nm | Invisible | Cannot be filtered | Salt in water, sugar in water |
| Colloid (colloidal solution) | 1–1000 nm | Invisible but shows Tyndall effect | Cannot filter; can ultrafiltrate | Milk, blood, butter, fog |
| Suspension | > 1000 nm | Visible (usually) | Can be filtered | Sand in water, chalk in water |

---

## 2. Tyndall Effect

> **Definition:** Scattering of light by colloidal particles when a beam of light passes through a colloid.

- **Shows:** Colloid has dispersed particles of appropriate size to scatter visible light
- **Doesn't show:** True solutions (particles too small)

**Examples of Tyndall Effect:**
- Beam of sunlight through dusty air (dust = colloid-like particles)
- Headlight beam visible in fog
- Light through a glass of milk
- Laser pointer through smoke

## 3. Types of Colloids

| Dispersed Phase | Dispersion Medium | Name | Example |
|----------------|-----------------|------|---------|
| Solid | Liquid | **Sol** | Blood (colloidal Fe(OH)₃), paint, ink |
| Liquid | Liquid | **Emulsion** | Milk (fat in water), mayonnaise, butter |
| Gas | Liquid | **Foam** | Shaving cream, whipped cream |
| Solid | Gas | **Aerosol** (solid) | Smoke, dust clouds |
| Liquid | Gas | **Aerosol** (liquid) | Fog, mist, clouds, spray |
| Gas | Solid | **Solid foam** | Pumice stone, foam rubber |
| Liquid | Solid | **Solid emulsion** (gel) | Butter, cheese, jelly, boot polish |
| Solid | Solid | **Solid sol** | Gemstones, coloured glass |

---

## 4. Colloidal Properties

| Property | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Tyndall effect | Light scattering |
| Brownian motion | Random zigzag motion of colloidal particles due to molecular bombardment |
| Electrophoresis | Movement of colloidal particles under electric field |
| Coagulation/precipitation | Addition of electrolyte destabilises colloid (aggregates) |
| Dialysis | Separation of colloid from small molecules using semi-permeable membrane |

---

## 5. Emulsions

| Type | Dispersed Phase | Medium | Example |
|------|----------------|--------|---------|
| O/W emulsion | Oil | Water | Milk, cold cream, mayonnaise |
| W/O emulsion | Water | Oil | Butter, margarine |

**Emulsifier (emulsifying agent):** Stabilises emulsion by reducing interfacial tension.
- Soap, lecithin, detergent, egg yolk (in mayonnaise)
