## 2. Circulatory System

### 2.1 Heart

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Chambers | 4: 2 atria (upper) + 2 ventricles (lower) |
| Heart beat rate | ~72 per minute at rest |
| Heart size | Approximately your fist |
| Location | Mediastinum (between lungs, slightly left) |
| Outer covering | Pericardium |
| Wall muscle | Myocardium |
| Inner lining | Endocardium |

**Chambers and blood flow:**
```
Right atrium ← deoxygenated blood from body (via superior/inferior vena cava)
Right atrium → Right ventricle (through tricuspid valve)
Right ventricle → Lungs (via pulmonary artery — deoxygenated blood)
Lungs → Left atrium (via pulmonary vein — oxygenated blood)
Left atrium → Left ventricle (through mitral/bicuspid valve)
Left ventricle → Body (via aorta — oxygenated blood)
```

### 2.2 Blood Vessels

| Vessel | Direction | Blood Type | Walls | Valves |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------|--------|
| **Arteries** | Away from heart | Oxygenated (EXCEPT pulmonary artery) | Thick, muscular | No valves |
| **Veins** | Toward heart | Deoxygenated (EXCEPT pulmonary vein) | Thinner | Have valves (prevent backflow) |
| **Capillaries** | Connect arteries to veins | Mixed | Very thin (1 cell thick) | No valves |

**CRITICAL EXCEPTIONS (PYQ favourite trap):**

| Vessel | Exception |
|--------|-----------|
| **Pulmonary artery** | Carries DEOXYGENATED blood (from heart to lungs) |
| **Pulmonary vein** | Carries OXYGENATED blood (from lungs to heart) |
| **Umbilical artery** (in fetus) | Carries deoxygenated blood |
| **Umbilical vein** (in fetus) | Carries oxygenated blood |

### 2.3 Blood Composition

| Component | % of Blood | Function |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Plasma | ~55% | Liquid part; transports nutrients, hormones, CO₂, waste; contains proteins |
| Red Blood Cells (RBC) / Erythrocytes | ~44% | Carry O₂ via haemoglobin; no nucleus (in mammals) |
| White Blood Cells (WBC) / Leukocytes | <1% | Immunity, fight infection; 5 types |
| Platelets / Thrombocytes | <1% | Blood clotting (with fibrin) |

### 2.4 Blood Groups — ABO System (Complete PYQ Table)

| Blood Group | Antigen on RBC | Antibody in Plasma | Can Donate To | Can Receive From |
|-------------|--------------|-------------------|--------------|-----------------|
| A | A | Anti-B | A, AB | A, O |
| B | B | Anti-A | B, AB | B, O |
| **AB** | A and B | **None** | **AB only** | **ALL (Universal Recipient)** |
| **O** | **None** | Anti-A, Anti-B | **ALL (Universal Donor)** | **O only** |

> **Universal Donor = O⁻ (O negative)**
> **Universal Recipient = AB+ (AB positive)**

**Rh factor:**
- **Rh positive (Rh⁺):** Rh antigen present on RBC (85% of population)
- **Rh negative (Rh⁻):** No Rh antigen
- **Rh incompatibility:** Rh⁻ mother + Rh⁺ fetus → mother makes anti-Rh antibodies → erythroblastosis fetalis in subsequent pregnancies

### 2.5 WBC Types and Functions

| WBC Type | % | Function |
|----------|---|---------|
| **Neutrophils** | 60–70% | First responders; phagocytose bacteria |
| **Lymphocytes** | 20–30% | Antibody production (B cells); kill infected cells (T cells) |
| **Monocytes** | 2–8% | Become macrophages; phagocytosis |
| **Eosinophils** | 1–4% | Combat parasites; allergic reactions |
| **Basophils** | 0.5–1% | Release histamine; allergic response |

## 3. Digestive System

### 3.1 Organs and Functions

| Organ | Function | Key Secretion/Enzyme |
|-------|---------|---------------------|
| **Mouth (buccal cavity)** | Mechanical digestion (chewing); chemical digestion begins | Salivary amylase (ptyalin) — starch → maltose |
| **Oesophagus (gullet)** | Transports food to stomach by peristalsis | No digestive enzymes; mucus |
| **Stomach** | Mechanical churning; protein digestion; kills bacteria | HCl (pH 1.5–2); Pepsin (protein → peptides); Rennin (milk protein in infants) |
| **Small intestine** | Main site of digestion AND absorption | See below |
| **Liver** | Bile production; glycogen storage; detoxification; protein metabolism | Bile (not enzyme) — emulsifies fats |
| **Gall bladder** | Stores bile | Bile (stored) |
| **Pancreas** | Exocrine: digestive enzymes; Endocrine: insulin/glucagon | Amylase, lipase, trypsin, chymotrypsin, nucleases |
| **Large intestine (colon)** | Water absorption; formation of faeces | — |
| **Rectum** | Storage of faeces | — |

### 3.2 Digestive Enzymes — Master Table

| Enzyme | Source | Substrate | Product | pH/Condition |
|--------|--------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Salivary amylase (ptyalin) | Salivary glands (mouth) | Starch | Maltose | pH ~7 (neutral) |
| Pepsin | Stomach | Proteins | Polypeptides | pH 1.5–2 (acidic) |
| Rennin (chymosin) | Stomach (infants) | Casein (milk protein) | Solid curd | pH ~2 |
| Pancreatic amylase | Pancreas | Starch | Maltose | pH ~8 |
| Trypsin | Pancreas | Proteins | Peptides | pH ~8 |
| Chymotrypsin | Pancreas | Proteins | Peptides | pH ~8 |
| Pancreatic lipase | Pancreas | Fats (after emulsification) | Fatty acids + glycerol | pH ~8 |
| Maltase | Small intestine | Maltose | Glucose | pH ~7 |
| Sucrase | Small intestine | Sucrose | Glucose + Fructose | pH ~7 |
| Lactase | Small intestine | Lactose (milk sugar) | Glucose + Galactose | pH ~7 |
| Bile (not enzyme) | Liver (stored in gall bladder) | Fats | Fat droplets (emulsification) | pH ~7–8 |

> **Memory:** "Salivary amylase in mouth cooks starch; stomach ACID + pepsin cook proteins; pancreas finishes everything; small intestine absorbs"

### 3.3 Absorption

- **Small intestine** is the main site of absorption (has villi and microvilli → increases surface area enormously)
- **Villi:** Finger-like projections; each contains capillaries (for sugars and amino acids) and lacteals (for fats)
- **Glucose and amino acids** → directly into blood (portal vein → liver)
- **Fats** → absorbed as chylomicrons into lymphatics (lacteals) → thoracic duct → blood

## 4. Respiratory System

### 4.1 Pathway of Air

```
Nose/Mouth → Pharynx → Larynx → Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli
```

### 4.2 Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Breathing (ventilation) | Physical process of inhalation and exhalation |
| Respiration | Chemical process — glucose + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O + energy (ATP) |
| Alveoli | Tiny air sacs (250–500 million in lungs); site of gas exchange |
| Tidal volume | Air moved per normal breath ≈ 0.5 L |
| Vital capacity | Maximum air moved in one breath ≈ 3.5–4.5 L |
| Total lung capacity | ~6 L |
| Residual volume | Air always remaining in lungs ≈ 1.2 L (cannot be expelled) |
| Haemoglobin | Iron-containing protein in RBC; carries O₂ (oxyhaemoglobin) and CO₂ |

### 4.3 Gas Exchange

| Gas | Partial pressure in alveolar air vs blood | Direction |
|-----|------------------------------------------|---------|
| O₂ | Higher in alveoli than blood → diffuses into blood | Alveoli → Blood |
| CO₂ | Higher in blood than alveoli → diffuses out | Blood → Alveoli |

## 5. Excretory System

### 5.1 Organs of Excretion

| Organ | What it excretes |
|-------|----------------|
| **Kidneys** | Urea, uric acid, excess salts and water (as urine) |
| **Lungs** | CO₂, water vapour |
| **Skin (sweat glands)** | Salts (NaCl), urea, water (as sweat) |
| **Liver** | Bile pigments (from haemoglobin breakdown); converts NH₃ to urea |

### 5.2 Kidney — Key Facts

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Functional unit | **Nephron** (~1 million per kidney) |
| Blood filtered/day | ~180 litres |
| Urine produced/day | ~1–1.5 litres |
| Urine composition | Water (95%), urea, creatinine, uric acid, salts |
| Main nitrogenous waste | **Urea** (humans) |

**Stages of urine formation:**
1. **Glomerular filtration** — blood pressure forces plasma into Bowman's capsule
2. **Selective reabsorption** — glucose, amino acids, water reabsorbed in tubules
3. **Tubular secretion** — additional waste substances secreted into tubules

## 8. Exam Traps

> ⚠️ **TRAP 1:** **Pulmonary artery carries DEOXYGENATED blood** (from heart to lungs). "Artery = away from heart" does NOT always mean oxygenated blood.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 2:** **Liver is largest INTERNAL organ; Skin is largest organ** — both are correct but in different categories.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 3:** **Bile does NOT contain enzymes** — bile emulsifies fats physically but doesn't chemically digest them.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 4:** **Stomach acid (HCl)** makes the stomach acidic — but pepsin is the enzyme that digests proteins. HCl activates pepsinogen → pepsin.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 5:** **O blood group can donate to all (Universal Donor)** but can ONLY receive from O. AB can receive from all (Universal Recipient) but can ONLY donate to AB.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 6:** **RBC has NO nucleus in adult mammals** — but fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds have nucleated RBCs.
