## 1. Photosynthesis — Core Reaction

```
6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
             (Chlorophyll)
         Glucose     Oxygen

Raw materials: CO₂ (from air via stomata) + H₂O (from soil via roots)
Products: Glucose (stored as starch) + O₂ (released as byproduct)
```

---

## 2. Where Does O₂ Come From? (PYQ Critical)

> **O₂ is released from the PHOTOLYSIS OF WATER, not from CO₂.**

Proof: Using isotope labelling (¹⁸O):
- When water (H₂¹⁸O) is used → O₂ produced contains ¹⁸O
- When CO₂ (C¹⁸O₂) is used → O₂ produced does NOT contain ¹⁸O

---

## 3. Site of Photosynthesis

| Component | Location | Function in Photosynthesis |
|-----------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Chloroplast** | Plant cells (mainly leaf mesophyll) | Entire photosynthesis occurs here |
| **Thylakoids** | Flattened membranes stacked (grana) inside chloroplast | Light reactions occur here |
| **Stroma** | Fluid surrounding thylakoids | Dark reactions (Calvin cycle) occur here |

---

## 4. Light Reactions (Photo Reactions)

**Location:** Thylakoid membranes (grana)

**Requirements:** Light energy, H₂O, NADP⁺, ADP+Pi

**Products:** ATP, NADPH, O₂

```
Process:
1. Light absorbed by chlorophyll → excites electrons
2. Photolysis of water: 2H₂O → 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ + O₂ (O₂ released here!)
3. Electrons pass through Electron Transport Chain → ATP synthesis
4. NADP⁺ + H⁺ + 2e⁻ → NADPH
```

---

## 5. Dark Reactions (Calvin Cycle — Carbon Fixation)

**Location:** Stroma of chloroplast

**Requirements:** CO₂, ATP, NADPH (from light reactions)

**Products:** G3P (Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate) → Glucose

```
Calvin Cycle steps:
1. Carbon fixation: CO₂ + RuBP (C5) → 2 × 3-PGA (C3) [by enzyme RuBisCO]
2. Reduction: 3-PGA → G3P (using ATP + NADPH)
3. Regeneration: G3P → RuBP (using ATP)
Net: 3CO₂ → 1 G3P (= ½ glucose)
```

**C3 plants vs C4 plants:**

| Feature | C3 Plants | C4 Plants |
|---------|----------|----------|
| First product | 3-PGA (3-carbon) | OAA (4-carbon) |
| CO₂ fixation | By RuBisCO in mesophyll | Bundle sheath cells |
| Efficiency | Less efficient in hot/dry | More efficient; less photorespiration |
| Examples | Rice, wheat, potato, soybean | Maize (corn), sugarcane, sorghum |

## 6. Chlorophyll and Light Absorption

| Pigment | Colour | Wavelengths Absorbed | PYQ |
|---------|--------|---------------------|-----|
| **Chlorophyll a** | Blue-green | Red + Blue | 🔥 |
| **Chlorophyll b** | Yellow-green | Red + Blue | ⭐ |
| **Carotenoids (β-carotene)** | Orange-yellow | Blue-violet | ⭐ |
| **Xanthophylls** | Yellow | Blue-violet | 📘 |

> **Chlorophyll reflects GREEN light** → plants appear green
> **Chlorophyll absorbs RED and BLUE wavelengths** most effectively

**Action spectrum:** Graph of photosynthesis rate vs wavelength — peaks at RED (~660 nm) and BLUE (~430 nm)
**Absorption spectrum:** Graph of light absorbed vs wavelength — similar peaks for chlorophyll

## 7. Factors Affecting Photosynthesis

| Factor | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Light intensity | Increases PS rate up to a saturation point; very high light may inhibit |
| CO₂ concentration | Increases PS rate up to saturation |
| Temperature | Increases up to ~35°C; then decreases (enzymes denature) |
| Water | Required; deficiency reduces PS (stomata close) |
| Chlorophyll amount | More chlorophyll = more PS (to a point) |

**Blackman's Law of Limiting Factors:** Rate of PS is limited by the factor that is most deficient.

## 8. Respiration in Plants

**Cellular respiration** occurs in ALL living cells (including plants) at ALL times (day and night):
```
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 38 ATP
```

**Comparison:**
- During DAYTIME: Photosynthesis rate > Respiration rate → net O₂ release, CO₂ uptake
- At **compensation point:** PS rate = Respiration rate → net gas exchange = 0
- At night: Only respiration → CO₂ released, O₂ consumed

---

## 9. Transpiration

**Definition:** Loss of water vapour from plant surfaces (mainly through stomata on leaves).

| Type | % of total water loss |
|------|----------------------|
| Stomatal transpiration | 90–95% (most) |
| Cuticular transpiration | 5–10% |
| Lenticular transpiration | <1% |

**Functions of transpiration:**
- Creates transpiration pull (COHESION-TENSION mechanism) → water rises in tall trees
- Cools the leaf (evaporative cooling)
- Facilitates mineral transport from roots to leaves

## 10. Plant Hormones (Phytohormones)

| Hormone | Main Site of Synthesis | Key Functions | Applications | PYQ |
|---------|----------------------|--------------|--------------|-----|
| **Auxin (IAA — Indole-3-acetic acid)** | Growing tips (shoot apex), young leaves | Cell elongation; phototropism (stem bends toward light); geotropism; apical dominance (suppresses lateral buds) | Root initiation in cuttings; herbicides (2,4-D) at high concentrations | 🔥 |
| **Gibberellins (GA)** | Young shoot, seeds, roots | Stem elongation (bolting); seed germination; flowering; fruit development | Malted barley (beer brewing); overcoming seed dormancy; increasing grape size | ⭐ |
| **Cytokinins (CK)** | Root tips, seeds, fruits | Cell division; delay of senescence (ageing) in leaves; shoot differentiation | Tissue culture; prevent post-harvest yellowing of leaves | ⭐ |
| **Abscisic acid (ABA)** | Leaves, stems, roots | Stomatal closure (drought response); seed dormancy; leaf abscission (leaf fall) | "Stress hormone" of plants | ⭐ |
| **Ethylene (C₂H₄)** | Ripening fruits, stressed tissues | Fruit ripening; **leaf/fruit/flower abscission**; inhibits stem elongation | **Artificial ripening of fruits** (bananas, mangoes, tomatoes); fruit storage (ethylene removed) | 🔥 |

> **Memory:** "**AGCAE**" → Auxin, Gibberellin, Cytokinin, ABA, Ethylene
> **Ethylene** = only GASEOUS plant hormone

## 11. Tropisms (Plant Movements)

| Tropism | Stimulus | Response | Plant Part |
|---------|---------|---------|-----------|
| Phototropism | Light | Positive (shoots bend toward light) | Shoots (auxin redistributes away from light side) |
| Geotropism (gravitropism) | Gravity | Positive roots; negative shoots | Roots grow down; shoots grow up |
| Hydrotropism | Water | Positive | Roots grow toward water |
| Thigmotropism | Touch | Wrapping around support | Tendrils of climbing plants |

## 12. Exam Traps

> ⚠️ **TRAP 1:** O₂ in photosynthesis comes from WATER (H₂O), NOT from CO₂. This is a classic trap.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 2:** Chlorophyll REFLECTS green light — that's why plants appear green. It ABSORBS red and blue.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 3:** Dark reactions are NOT completely independent of light — they require ATP and NADPH made during light reactions. They just don't need light DIRECTLY.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 4:** Ethylene is the ONLY gaseous plant hormone — it is a gas at room temperature.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 5:** ABA causes stomata to CLOSE (under drought/stress conditions) — NOT to open.
