## 2. Vitamins — Complete Master Table

### 2.1 Fat-Soluble Vitamins (A, D, E, K)

> **Memory:** "**ADEK**" = fat-soluble → "**A Dog Eats Kibble**"
> Stored in fat tissue and liver; can cause toxicity if excess

| Vitamin | Chemical Name | Deficiency Disease | Symptoms | Source | PYQ |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|---------|--------|-----|
| **A** | **Retinol** | **Night blindness; Xerophthalmia; Keratomalacia** | Poor vision in dim light; dry eyes; corneal damage | Carrots (β-carotene, provitamin A), dairy, egg yolk, liver, fish oil, yellow/orange fruits | 🔥 |
| **D** | **Calciferol** (D₂ = ergocalciferol; D₃ = cholecalciferol) | **Rickets** (children); **Osteomalacia** (adults); Osteoporosis (elderly) | Soft, deformed bones in children; bone pain in adults | Sunlight (skin synthesis — MOST IMPORTANT); fish oil (cod liver oil), fortified milk, egg yolk | 🔥 |
| **E** | **Tocopherol** | Sterility; muscle weakness; nerve damage | Infertility; haemolytic anaemia in newborns | Vegetable oils, nuts, seeds, green leafy vegetables | ⭐ |
| **K** | **Phylloquinone** (K₁ from plants); **Menaquinone** (K₂ from bacteria) | Impaired blood clotting; excessive/prolonged bleeding | Haemorrhage; slow wound healing | Green leafy vegetables (spinach, kale), liver, bacterial synthesis in gut | 🔥 |

### 2.2 Water-Soluble Vitamins (B Complex + C)

> Water-soluble vitamins are NOT stored in body; excess is excreted in urine; less risk of toxicity but deficiency develops faster.

| Vitamin | Chemical Name | Deficiency Disease | Key Symptoms | Source | PYQ |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|-------------|--------|-----|
| **B1** | **Thiamine** | **Beriberi** | Wet beriberi (heart/oedema); Dry beriberi (peripheral nerve damage, muscle wasting) | Cereals, nuts, legumes, yeast, pork | 🔥 |
| **B2** | **Riboflavin** | **Ariboflavinosis** | Cracked lips/corners of mouth (cheilosis), inflamed tongue, skin rash | Milk, eggs, meat, green vegetables | ⭐ |
| **B3** | **Niacin** (Nicotinic acid) | **Pellagra** | "4D's": Dermatitis, Diarrhoea, Dementia, Death | Meat, fish, nuts, cereals, legumes | 🔥 |
| **B5** | **Pantothenic acid** | Burning feet syndrome; fatigue | Burning/tingling feet, fatigue, irritability | Widespread (most foods) | 📘 |
| **B6** | **Pyridoxine** | Anaemia; convulsions; peripheral neuropathy | Confusion, depression, dermatitis | Meat, fish, potatoes, bananas, chickpeas | ⭐ |
| **B7** | **Biotin** (Vitamin H) | Dermatitis; hair loss; neurological problems | Brittle nails, hair loss, skin rash | Eggs (whites BLOCK biotin!), nuts, seeds | 📘 |
| **B9** | **Folic acid** (Folate) | **Megaloblastic anaemia; Neural tube defects** (in fetus: spina bifida, anencephaly) | Large, immature RBCs; birth defects | Green leafy vegetables, legumes, citrus, liver | 🔥 |
| **B12** | **Cobalamin** (contains cobalt!) | **Pernicious anaemia; Subacute combined degeneration of spinal cord** | Megaloblastic anaemia; neurological damage | **ONLY in animal products:** meat, fish, dairy, eggs. ABSENT in plants | 🔥 |
| **C** | **Ascorbic acid** | **Scurvy** | Bleeding gums (most characteristic), loose teeth, skin haemorrhages, poor wound healing | Citrus fruits (lemon, orange), amla (Indian gooseberry — highest), guava, tomato, bell pepper | 🔥 |

## 4. Minerals — Complete Deficiency Table

| Mineral | Role | Deficiency Disease/Effect | Source | PYQ |
|---------|------|--------------------------|--------|-----|
| **Iron (Fe)** | Haemoglobin and myoglobin; oxygen transport; enzyme cofactor | **Anaemia** (iron-deficiency anaemia) — fatigue, pale skin, weakness | Spinach, meat, beans, fortified cereals, liver | 🔥 |
| **Calcium (Ca)** | Bones and teeth (99% of body's Ca); blood clotting; muscle contraction; nerve function | **Rickets** (children with Vit D); **Osteoporosis** (elderly, esp women); **Tetany** (muscle spasms) | Milk, cheese, yogurt, leafy greens, fish bones | 🔥 |
| **Iodine (I)** | Synthesis of thyroid hormones (thyroxine T4, triiodothyronine T3) | **Goitre** (enlarged thyroid gland); **Cretinism** (congenital hypothyroidism in infants — stunted growth, intellectual disability) | Iodised salt (most important), seafood, dairy | 🔥 |
| **Phosphorus (P)** | Bones, teeth; ATP; DNA/RNA; cell membranes | Rickets (with Ca); muscle weakness | Meat, dairy, nuts, beans | 📘 |
| **Magnesium (Mg)** | Enzyme cofactor; bone; heart rhythm | Muscle cramps, irregular heartbeat | Green vegetables, nuts, legumes | 📘 |
| **Sodium (Na)** | Fluid balance; nerve impulse; blood pressure | **Hyponatraemia** — muscle cramps, confusion; excess causes hypertension | Salt, vegetables, meat | ⭐ |
| **Potassium (K)** | Heart rhythm; fluid balance; nerve/muscle function | **Hypokalaemia** — muscle weakness, arrhythmia | Banana, potato, orange, legumes | ⭐ |
| **Zinc (Zn)** | 100+ enzyme functions; immune system; wound healing; growth | Growth retardation; skin problems; impaired immunity; hypogeusia (loss of taste) | Meat, shellfish, nuts, dairy | ⭐ |
| **Fluoride (F)** | Tooth enamel hardening | **Dental caries** (cavities) | Fluoridated water, tea, seafood | ⭐ |
| **Fluoride (excess)** | — | **Fluorosis** (mottled, discoloured teeth; skeletal fluorosis) | Excess fluoride in water (especially groundwater in fluoride-rich areas) | 🔥 |
| **Copper (Cu)** | Haemoglobin synthesis; enzyme cofactor; melanin | Anaemia; bone problems | Liver, nuts, seafood | 📘 |
| **Selenium (Se)** | Antioxidant enzymes; thyroid function | Keshan disease (heart); Kashin-Beck disease (joints) | Brazil nuts, seafood, meat | 📘 |
| **Manganese (Mn)** | Enzyme cofactor; bone formation | Bone malformations; impaired growth | Nuts, whole grains, leafy vegetables | 📘 |

## 5. Macronutrients

| Macronutrient | Function | Caloric Value | Major Sources |
|--------------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| **Carbohydrates** | Primary energy source; fibre for digestion | **4 kcal/g** | Rice, wheat, maize, sugar, potato |
| **Proteins** | Growth, repair, enzymes, hormones, antibodies | **4 kcal/g** | Meat, eggs, fish, milk, legumes, soybean |
| **Fats (Lipids)** | Energy storage; insulation; fat-soluble vitamin absorption; hormones | **9 kcal/g** | Oil, butter, nuts, meat, dairy |

> **Memory:** "**Fats are FATTY = 9; Proteins and Carbs = 4 each**" (F9 > PC4)

### 5.1 Types of Carbohydrates

| Type | Example | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Monosaccharides | Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆), fructose, galactose | Simplest sugars; directly absorbed |
| Disaccharides | Sucrose (table sugar), maltose, lactose | 2 monosaccharides; split by enzymes |
| Polysaccharides | Starch, glycogen, cellulose | Complex; starch = energy storage in plants; glycogen = in animals/liver |

### 5.2 Types of Proteins

| Type | Function | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Structural | Build/repair tissues | Collagen, keratin, actin |
| Enzymatic | Catalyse reactions | Amylase, pepsin, catalase |
| Transport | Carry substances | Haemoglobin (O₂), albumin |
| Hormones | Chemical signalling | Insulin, glucagon |
| Antibodies | Immune function | IgG, IgM |
| Essential amino acids | Must be obtained from diet (9 types) | Leucine, valine, tryptophan... |

### 5.3 Types of Fats

| Type | Double bonds | Examples | Health |
|------|-------------|---------|--------|
| Saturated | 0 | Animal fat, butter, coconut oil | Raises LDL cholesterol |
| Monounsaturated | 1 | Olive oil, avocado | Heart-healthy |
| Polyunsaturated | 2+ | Fish oil (omega-3), sunflower oil | Heart-healthy |
| Trans fat | Artificial | Margarine, partially hydrogenated oils | Harmful — raises LDL, lowers HDL |

## 6. Water

- Often called the "**universal solvent**"
- Essential for: metabolism, temperature regulation, transport, digestion
- Makes up ~60–70% of human body weight
- Daily requirement: ~2–3 litres (varies with climate, activity)
- Dehydration: > 1% loss → thirst; > 10% → dangerous

---

## 7. Balanced Diet

**A balanced diet provides:**
- Adequate carbohydrates (~55–60% of energy)
- Adequate proteins (~10–15% of energy)
- Adequate fats (~25–30% of energy)
- All essential vitamins and minerals
- Adequate fibre (25–30 g/day)
- Adequate water

## 8. Exam Traps

> ⚠️ **TRAP 1:** Rickets is caused by **Vitamin D deficiency** (NOT calcium deficiency alone). Even with adequate dietary calcium, lack of Vitamin D prevents proper calcium absorption.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 2:** **Scurvy** = Vitamin C deficiency (NOT Vitamin A). Vitamin A deficiency = Night blindness.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 3:** **Vitamin B12 is found ONLY in animal products** (meat, fish, dairy, eggs). Strict vegans MUST supplement.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 4:** **Goitre** is caused by **IODINE deficiency** (it is a mineral deficiency, NOT a vitamin deficiency). Thyroid needs iodine for thyroxine synthesis.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 5:** **Vitamin D** is technically a **steroid hormone** synthesised by skin in sunlight. Sun exposure is the most important source, not diet.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 6:** **Fluoride EXCESS** causes **fluorosis**; fluoride DEFICIENCY causes dental caries (cavities). The effect goes both ways.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 7:** **Vitamin K** is needed for blood CLOTTING. Deficiency = excessive bleeding (NOT increased clotting risk).

> ⚠️ **TRAP 8:** **Folic acid (B9)** is critical during pregnancy for preventing neural tube defects (spina bifida). Women are advised to start taking folic acid BEFORE conception.
