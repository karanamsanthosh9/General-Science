## 2. Viral Diseases — Complete PYQ Master Table

| Disease | Causative Virus | Transmission | Vector | Affects | Key Fact | PYQ |
|---------|----------------|-------------|--------|---------|---------|-----|
| **AIDS** | HIV (Human Immunodeficiency Virus) — Retrovirus | Blood transfusion, sexual contact, needle sharing, mother to child (vertical) | None | Immune system (CD4 T-lymphocytes) | Destroys CD4 T-cells → immunosuppression; no cure; antiretroviral therapy (ART) | 🔥 |
| **Influenza (Flu)** | Influenza virus (A, B, C) | Air droplets, contact | None | Respiratory tract | Causes epidemics/pandemics (e.g., Spanish flu 1918); annual vaccination needed (strain changes) | 🔥 |
| **Dengue fever** | Dengue virus (Flavivirus, 4 serotypes) | **Aedes aegypti** mosquito bite | Aedes aegypti (day-biting) | Blood, platelets drop | Thrombocytopenia (platelet count drops drastically); no specific antiviral; severe form = dengue haemorrhagic fever | 🔥 |
| **Chickenpox** | Varicella-zoster virus (VZV) | Air droplets, contact with lesions | None | Skin, nervous system | Characteristic vesicular rash all over body; VZV reactivates as Shingles (Herpes zoster) in elderly | 🔥 |
| **Smallpox** | Variola virus (Variola major/minor) | Direct contact, air droplets | None | Skin, systemic | **ERADICATED globally in 1980 (WHO declaration)** — last natural case 1977; world's first eradicated disease | 🔥 |
| **Poliomyelitis (Polio)** | Poliovirus (3 serotypes) | **Faecal-oral route** (contaminated water/food) | None | Motor neurons → paralysis | Salk vaccine (IPV = killed virus); Sabin vaccine (OPV = live attenuated oral); India declared polio-free 2014 | 🔥 |
| **Rabies** | Rabies virus (Lyssavirus, Rhabdovirus family) | **Animal bite** (dog, bat, fox, raccoon) | None | Brain, nervous system | Hydrophobia (fear of water); fatal if not treated; post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) with rabies vaccine | 🔥 |
| **Hepatitis A** | Hepatitis A virus (HAV) | Contaminated water/food (faecal-oral) | None | Liver | Self-limiting; vaccine available | ⭐ |
| **Hepatitis B** | Hepatitis B virus (HBV) | Blood, sexual contact, mother to child | None | Liver | Chronic infection → cirrhosis, liver cancer; effective vaccine available | 🔥 |
| **Hepatitis C** | Hepatitis C virus (HCV) | Blood, sexual contact | None | Liver | No vaccine; curable with antivirals | ⭐ |
| **Measles** | Measles virus (Paramyxovirus) | Air droplets (highly contagious) | None | Respiratory, skin | Koplik's spots (white spots in mouth) characteristic; MMR vaccine | ⭐ |
| **Mumps** | Mumps virus (Paramyxovirus) | Air droplets | None | Parotid glands (swelling) | Painful swelling of jaw/cheek; MMR vaccine | ⭐ |
| **COVID-19** | SARS-CoV-2 (Coronavirus) | Respiratory droplets, aerosols | None | Lungs, multiple organs | Pandemic 2020; mRNA vaccines (Pfizer, Moderna), viral vector vaccines | ⭐ |
| **Japanese Encephalitis** | Japanese Encephalitis virus | **Culex mosquito** | Culex | Brain | Encephalitis (brain inflammation); vaccine available | ⭐ |
| **Chikungunya** | Chikungunya virus (Togavirus) | **Aedes aegypti/albopictus** | Aedes | Joints (severe arthralgia) | Joint pain can persist for months; no vaccine | ⭐ |
| **Zika fever** | Zika virus (Flavivirus) | **Aedes aegypti** | Aedes | Developing fetus (microcephaly) | Causes microcephaly in newborns; sexual transmission also possible | ⭐ |
| **Ebola** | Ebola virus (Filovirus) | Direct contact with body fluids | None | Multiple organs | Haemorrhagic fever; high mortality; outbreaks in Africa | 📘 |

## 3. Bacterial Diseases — Complete PYQ Master Table

| Disease | Causative Bacterium | Transmission | Affects | Key Features | Vaccine/Treatment | PYQ |
|---------|--------------------|-----------| -------|------------|------------------|-----|
| **Tuberculosis (TB)** | *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* | Air droplets | Lungs (pulmonary TB) | Chronic cough, blood in sputum, night sweats, weight loss | **BCG vaccine**; anti-TB drugs (DOTS — Directly Observed Treatment Short-course): isoniazid, rifampicin, etc. | 🔥 |
| **Typhoid fever** | *Salmonella typhi* | Contaminated food and water (faecal-oral) | Digestive system; intestines | Sustained fever (rose spots on abdomen), headache, diarrhoea/constipation; "Widal test" for diagnosis | TAB vaccine; antibiotics (ciprofloxacin) | 🔥 |
| **Cholera** | *Vibrio cholerae* | Contaminated water (faecal-oral) | Small intestine | Profuse watery diarrhoea ("rice-water stools"); severe dehydration; rapidly fatal if untreated | ORS (oral rehydration); antibiotics; cholera vaccine | 🔥 |
| **Plague** | *Yersinia pestis* | Flea bite (fleas from infected **rats**) | Lymph nodes, blood, lungs | Bubonic plague (swollen lymph nodes = buboes); Pneumonic plague (lungs); Black Death | Antibiotics (streptomycin); no routine vaccine | 🔥 |
| **Leprosy (Hansen's disease)** | *Mycobacterium leprae* | Prolonged direct contact | Skin, peripheral nerves | Loss of sensation, skin lesions, nerve damage; NOT highly contagious | Multi-drug therapy (MDT): dapsone, rifampicin, clofazimine; India has achieved elimination | 🔥 |
| **Tetanus (Lockjaw)** | *Clostridium tetani* | Spores in soil entering wounds | Nervous system | **Lockjaw** (jaw muscle spasms), body rigidity, convulsions; spores germinate in anaerobic wounds | **DPT/Td vaccine**; tetanus antitoxin; clean wounds | 🔥 |
| **Diphtheria** | *Corynebacterium diphtheriae* | Air droplets, contact | Throat, upper airway | Thick grey membrane forms in throat; "bull neck" (lymph node swelling); heart/nerve damage from toxin | **DPT vaccine** | ⭐ |
| **Whooping cough (Pertussis)** | *Bordetella pertussis* | Air droplets | Respiratory tract | Severe paroxysmal cough with characteristic "whoop" sound; dangerous for infants | **DPT vaccine** | ⭐ |
| **Pneumonia** | *Streptococcus pneumoniae* (most common) | Air droplets | Lungs | Fever, cough, difficulty breathing; fluid in lungs | Pneumococcal vaccine; antibiotics | ⭐ |
| **Meningitis (bacterial)** | *Neisseria meningitidis*, *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Haemophilus influenzae* | Air droplets | Brain membranes | Stiff neck, fever, headache; petechial rash (meningococcal) | Vaccines; antibiotics urgently | ⭐ |
| **Gonorrhoea** | *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* | Sexual contact (STI) | Genitals, urethra | Painful urination, discharge | Antibiotics | 📘 |
| **Syphilis** | *Treponema pallidum* | Sexual contact (STI) | Genitals, systemic | Primary chancre → secondary rash → tertiary (heart, brain) | Penicillin | 📘 |
| **Food poisoning** | *Staphylococcus aureus*, *Salmonella*, *Clostridium botulinum* | Contaminated food | Digestive system | Nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea | Supportive; antibiotics if severe | ⭐ |

## 4. Parasitic & Protozoal Diseases — Complete PYQ Master Table

| Disease | Causative Organism | Type | Vector | Transmission | Key Features | PYQ |
|---------|-------------------|------|--------|-------------|-------------|-----|
| **Malaria** | *Plasmodium* species (P. falciparum — most dangerous; P. vivax — most common in India) | Protozoan (Apicomplexa) | **Female Anopheles mosquito** | Mosquito bite | Cyclic fever (every 48 hr for vivax; 48 hr falciparum; 72 hr malariae); spleen enlargement; chloroquine/artemisinin treatment | 🔥 |
| **Filariasis (Elephantiasis)** | *Wuchereria bancrofti* | Nematode (roundworm) | **Culex mosquito** | Mosquito bite | Lymph vessel blockage → massive swelling of limbs (elephantiasis) | 🔥 |
| **Kala-azar (Visceral leishmaniasis)** | *Leishmania donovani* | Protozoan (Kinetoplastida) | **Sandfly** (*Phlebotomus* species) | Sandfly bite | Fever, spleen and liver enlargement, weight loss, skin darkening | ⭐ |
| **Sleeping sickness** | *Trypanosoma brucei* | Protozoan | **Tsetse fly** | Tsetse fly bite | Neurological symptoms; sleep disorder; Africa | 📘 |
| **Chagas disease** | *Trypanosoma cruzi* | Protozoan | **Triatomine bug** (kissing bug) | Bug feces in wound/eye | Heart damage; Latin America | 📘 |
| **Amoebiasis (Amoebic dysentery)** | *Entamoeba histolytica* | Protozoan | No insect vector | Contaminated water/food | Bloody diarrhoea; liver abscess | ⭐ |
| **Giardiasis** | *Giardia lamblia* | Protozoan | No insect vector | Contaminated water | Diarrhoea, malabsorption | 📘 |
| **Tapeworm infection (Taeniasis/Cysticercosis)** | *Taenia solium* (pork) or *T. saginata* (beef) | Cestode (flatworm) | None | Eating undercooked meat | Intestinal; cysticercosis if larvae in brain | ⭐ |
| **Ascariasis (Roundworm)** | *Ascaris lumbricoides* | Nematode | None | Faecal-oral (contaminated soil/food) | Intestinal obstruction; worms in stool | ⭐ |
| **Hookworm infection** | *Ancylostoma duodenale*, *Necator americanus* | Nematode | None | Larvae penetrate skin (walking barefoot on contaminated soil) | Anaemia; intestinal blood loss | ⭐ |
| **Schistosomiasis (Bilharzia)** | *Schistosoma* species | Trematode (fluke) | Water snail (intermediate host) | Skin contact with infected water | Liver and bladder damage | 📘 |
| **Toxoplasmosis** | *Toxoplasma gondii* | Protozoan | None | Cat feces, undercooked meat | Usually asymptomatic; dangerous for fetus | 📘 |

## 8. Antibiotics and Antimicrobials

| Antibiotic | Source | Spectrum | Notable Use |
|-----------|--------|---------|------------|
| **Penicillin** | *Penicillium notatum* (fungus); Fleming 1928 | Gram-positive bacteria | First antibiotic; strep throat, syphilis, anthrax |
| **Streptomycin** | *Streptomyces griseus* | Broad; TB | First antibiotic for TB |
| **Tetracycline** | *Streptomyces* | Broad spectrum | Many bacterial infections |
| **Chloramphenicol** | *Streptomyces venezuelae* | Broad | Typhoid (now limited due to toxicity) |
| **Erythromycin** | *Streptomyces erythraeus* | Gram-positive | Alternative to penicillin |
| **Ciprofloxacin** | Synthetic (fluoroquinolone) | Broad | Typhoid, UTI, respiratory |

> **CRITICAL:** Antibiotics work ONLY against BACTERIA. They are NOT effective against viruses, fungi, or parasites.

## 9. Immunology Basics

**Types of Immunity:**

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Active immunity** | Body produces its own antibodies after exposure | Natural infection; vaccination |
| **Passive immunity** | Antibodies given externally; short-lived | Antiserum injection; mother's milk (IgA) |
| **Innate immunity** | Non-specific; first line of defence | Skin, mucus, phagocytes |
| **Adaptive immunity** | Specific; memory | B cells (antibodies), T cells |

## 10. Exam Traps

> ⚠️ **TRAP 1:** **Dengue is caused by a VIRUS** (not a bacterium or protozoan), transmitted by **Aedes aegypti** (NOT Anopheles).

> ⚠️ **TRAP 2:** **Malaria is caused by Plasmodium** (protozoan parasite), NOT by the mosquito itself. The mosquito is only the VECTOR.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 3:** **Female Anopheles transmits malaria** — NOT male Anopheles (males don't bite humans; they feed on nectar).

> ⚠️ **TRAP 4:** **Smallpox was ERADICATED in 1980** — it no longer exists naturally. The only known samples are kept in labs (CDC, USA and VECTOR, Russia).

> ⚠️ **TRAP 5:** **Antibiotics are effective ONLY against bacteria** — NOT viruses. Taking antibiotics for viral infections (cold, flu) is useless and creates antibiotic resistance.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 6:** **Edward Jenner created the SMALLPOX vaccine** (using cowpox, 1796). **Louis Pasteur** created the RABIES vaccine (1885).

> ⚠️ **TRAP 7:** **BCG vaccine is for TB**, not smallpox. BCG = Bacillus Calmette-Guérin.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 8:** **Leprosy is NOT highly contagious** — it requires prolonged close contact; most people are naturally immune.
