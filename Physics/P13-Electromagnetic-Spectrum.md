> **Exam Importance:** The electromagnetic spectrum — arrangement of EM waves by wavelength/frequency, and properties/uses of each type — is a moderate-yield topic. Knowing which radiation is used for which purpose is the key exam deliverable.

## 1. Properties of All EM Waves

- All EM waves are **transverse** in nature
- Travel at speed of light: **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** in vacuum
- Do NOT require any medium to propagate
- Obey wave equation: c = fλ
- Carry energy (E = hf)
- Can be reflected, refracted, diffracted, polarised

---

## 2. The Electromagnetic Spectrum

Arranged from LONGEST wavelength (lowest frequency) to SHORTEST wavelength (highest frequency):

```
Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible → Ultraviolet → X-ray → Gamma
(longest λ, lowest f)                                  (shortest λ, highest f)
```

| EM Wave | Wavelength Range | Frequency Range | Source | Uses |
|---------|----------------|----------------|--------|------|
| **Radio waves** | > 1 mm (1 m – 10⁴ km) | 3 Hz – 3 GHz | Radio transmitters, lightning | AM/FM radio, TV broadcasting, MRI |
| **Microwaves** | 1 mm – 1 m | 300 MHz – 300 GHz | Magnetron, klystron | Microwave oven, RADAR, mobile phones, satellite communication |
| **Infrared (IR)** | 700 nm – 1 mm | 3 × 10¹¹ – 4 × 10¹⁴ Hz | Warm objects | Remote controls, night vision, thermal imaging, physiotherapy, optical fibre, greenhouse effect |
| **Visible light** | 400 nm – 700 nm | 4 × 10¹⁴ – 7 × 10¹⁴ Hz | Hot bodies, sun | Vision, photography, optical instruments |
| **Ultraviolet (UV)** | 10 nm – 400 nm | 7.5 × 10¹⁴ – 3 × 10¹⁶ Hz | Sun, mercury arc lamp | Sterilisation, fluorescence, Vitamin D synthesis, water purification, detecting fake currency |
| **X-rays** | 0.01 nm – 10 nm | 3 × 10¹⁶ – 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz | X-ray tube, synchrotron | Medical imaging, security scanning, crystal structure determination |
| **Gamma rays** | < 0.01 nm | > 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz | Radioactive nuclei, cosmic | Cancer treatment (radiotherapy), sterilisation of medical equipment, food preservation |

## 5. Exam Traps

> ⚠️ **TRAP 1:** All EM waves travel at the SAME speed (3 × 10⁸ m/s) in vacuum — they differ in wavelength and frequency, NOT speed.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 2:** Infrared = HEAT radiation; it is NOT visible light. Remote controls use IR.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 3:** Gamma rays and X-rays both have short wavelengths but different SOURCES — gamma from nuclear decay; X-rays from electron deceleration.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 4:** UV causes skin cancer and blindness (cataracts) — it is BLOCKED by the ozone layer. Ozone depletion → more UV reaches Earth.
