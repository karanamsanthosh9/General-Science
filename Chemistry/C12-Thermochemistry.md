## 1. Thermochemistry Basics

**Thermochemistry** studies heat changes in chemical reactions.

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| System | Part of universe under study |
| Surroundings | Everything outside the system |
| State function | Property depending only on current state, not path (e.g., enthalpy, entropy) |
| Enthalpy (H) | Heat content at constant pressure |
| ΔH | Change in enthalpy = H_products − H_reactants |

---

## 2. Exothermic vs Endothermic

| Type | ΔH | Energy | Examples |
|------|----|--------|---------|
| **Exothermic** | Negative (ΔH < 0) | Released to surroundings; T increases | Combustion, neutralisation, respiration, dissolution of H₂SO₄ in water |
| **Endothermic** | Positive (ΔH > 0) | Absorbed from surroundings; T decreases | Photosynthesis, thermal decomposition, dissolution of NH₄NO₃ |

---

## 3. Enthalpy Terms

| Term | Symbol | Definition | Example |
|------|--------|-----------|---------|
| Enthalpy of combustion | ΔH_c | Heat released when 1 mole burns completely in O₂ | ΔH_c of CH₄ = −890 kJ/mol |
| Enthalpy of formation | ΔH_f | Heat change when 1 mole formed from elements | ΔH_f of H₂O = −286 kJ/mol |
| Enthalpy of neutralisation | ΔH_n | Heat when 1 mole H⁺ + 1 mole OH⁻ → H₂O | −57.3 kJ/mol (strong acid + strong base) |
| Enthalpy of solution | ΔH_sol | Heat when 1 mole dissolves in solvent | NaOH dissolution = exothermic |
| Enthalpy of vaporisation | ΔH_vap | Heat to vaporise 1 mole | H₂O: +40.7 kJ/mol |
| Enthalpy of fusion | ΔH_fus | Heat to melt 1 mole | Ice: +6.01 kJ/mol |

---

## 4. Hess's Law

> *"The total enthalpy change of a reaction is the same regardless of whether the reaction proceeds in one step or multiple steps — it depends only on the initial and final states."*

```
ΔH_reaction = ΣΔH_f(products) − ΣΔH_f(reactants)
```

**Application:** Calculate enthalpy of reactions that cannot be measured directly.

---

## 5. Bond Energy

- **Bond enthalpy:** Energy required to break 1 mole of a particular bond in the gaseous state (endothermic)
- **Bond dissociation enthalpy** values (approximate):

| Bond | Bond Energy (kJ/mol) |
|------|---------------------|
| H-H | 435 |
| O=O | 498 |
| C-H | 413 |
| C-C | 347 |
| C=C | 614 |
| C≡C | 839 |
| N≡N | 945 (very strong) |
| O-H | 460 |
| H-F | 569 |

```
ΔH_reaction = Σ(bond energies broken) − Σ(bond energies formed)
```

---

## 6. Calorimetry

**Calorimeter:** Instrument to measure heat of reactions.

```
q = mcΔT    (heat absorbed/released by calorimeter)
Heat of reaction = −q_calorimeter
```

**Standard conditions:** 25°C (298 K), 1 atm, 1 M concentration.

---

## 7. Laws of Thermodynamics (Chemical Context)

| Law | Chemical Significance |
|-----|----------------------|
| 1st Law | ΔU = q + w; energy conserved in reactions |
| 2nd Law | Spontaneous reactions increase total entropy |
| Gibbs Free Energy | ΔG = ΔH − TΔS; if ΔG < 0, reaction is spontaneous |
