# C15 — Industrial Chemistry 📖 Priority 4

> **Exam Importance:** Industrial processes (Haber process, Contact process, Solvay process, Chlor-alkali, Blast furnace) and their conditions are occasionally tested. The conditions (temperature, pressure, catalyst) are the key exam deliverables.

---

## 1. Haber Process (Ammonia Synthesis)

```
N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃    ΔH = −92 kJ/mol (exothermic)

Conditions:
- Temperature: 450–500°C (compromise between rate and yield)
- Pressure: 200–300 atm (high pressure favours product formation)
- Catalyst: Iron (Fe) with promoters K₂O and Al₂O₃
```

**Significance:**
- Produces **ammonia (NH₃)** — most important industrial chemical
- Used for: fertilisers (urea, ammonium nitrate), explosives, nitric acid, cleaning products
- **Fritz Haber** and **Carl Bosch** developed this process (Nobel Prizes)

---

## 2. Ostwald Process (Nitric Acid Synthesis)

```
Step 1: 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O     (Pt-Rh catalyst, 800-900°C)
Step 2: 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂
Step 3: 3NO₂ + H₂O → 2HNO₃ + NO
```

**Significance:** Industrial production of **nitric acid (HNO₃)** for fertilisers and explosives.

---

## 3. Contact Process (Sulphuric Acid Synthesis)

```
Step 1: S + O₂ → SO₂ (or FeS₂ → Fe₂O₃ + SO₂)
Step 2: 2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃     (V₂O₅ catalyst, 450°C, 1–2 atm)
Step 3: SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇ (oleum)
Step 4: H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄
```

**Significance:** H₂SO₄ is the most produced industrial chemical. Used in fertilisers, batteries, petroleum refining, dyes, paper.

---

## 4. Solvay Process (Sodium Carbonate / Washing Soda)

```
NaCl + NH₃ + CO₂ + H₂O → NaHCO₃ + NH₄Cl
2NaHCO₃ (heat) → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂
```

**Significance:** Industrial production of **Na₂CO₃ (washing soda)** and **NaHCO₃ (baking soda)**.

---

## 5. Chlor-Alkali Process (Electrolysis of Brine)

```
2NaCl + 2H₂O → Cl₂ + H₂ + 2NaOH

At cathode: 2H₂O + 2e⁻ → H₂ + 2OH⁻  (hydrogen gas)
At anode: 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻          (chlorine gas)
```

**Products:** Cl₂ (bleaching, PVC), H₂ (fuel, ammonia), NaOH (caustic soda — soap, paper)

---

## 6. Blast Furnace (Iron Extraction)

```
Charge: Iron ore (Fe₂O₃) + Coke (C) + Limestone (CaCO₃)

Key reactions:
1. C + O₂ → CO₂
2. CO₂ + C → 2CO   (reducing agent)
3. Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂   (iron reduction)
4. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂   (limestone decomposes)
5. CaO + SiO₂ → CaSiO₃  (slag formation — removes impurities)

Products: 
- Pig iron (molten iron + ~4% carbon)
- Slag (CaSiO₃) — used in road making, cement
```

---

## 7. Aluminium Extraction (Hall-Héroult Process)

```
Al₂O₃ (in cryolite melt Na₃AlF₆) → 2Al + 3O [by electrolysis at 950°C]

At cathode: Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al (aluminium deposited)
At anode: 2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻ (oxygen released; reacts with C anode → CO₂)
```
