## 1. Why Carbon is Special (Unique Properties)

| Property | Description | Significance |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Catenation** | Carbon can form long chains, branches, rings by bonding with itself | Basis of millions of organic compounds |
| **Tetravalency** | 4 valence electrons → 4 bonds | Can bond with H, O, N, S, halogens and itself |
| **Small atomic size** | Strong C-C bonds | Stable long chains possible |
| **Multiple bonding** | Can form single, double, triple bonds | Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes |
| **Bond with non-metals** | C-H, C-O, C-N, C-S bonds | Organic chemistry diversity |

## 2. Allotropes of Carbon

> **Allotropy:** Same element existing in different physical forms with different properties due to different arrangements of atoms.

| Allotrope | Structure | Key Properties | Uses |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|------|
| **Diamond** | Each C bonded to 4 others (3D tetrahedral network, sp³) | Hardest natural substance; transparent; excellent thermal conductor; electrical insulator | Cutting/drilling tools, jewellery, abrasives, heat sinks |
| **Graphite** | Layers of hexagonal rings; each C bonded to 3 others (sp²); free electron in each layer | Soft (layers slide); dark grey; excellent electrical conductor; good lubricant; opaque | Pencils, electrodes (batteries/electrolysis), lubricant, crucibles |
| **Fullerene (C₆₀)** | Spherical cage of 60 carbon atoms (soccer ball shape); sp² | Semiconducting; can hold other atoms inside | Drug delivery, nanotechnology, lubricants |
| **Carbon nanotubes (CNT)** | Cylindrical rolled graphene sheet | Stronger than steel; excellent conductor; lightweight | Electronics, composites, nano-devices |
| **Graphene** | Single layer of graphite (1 atom thick) | Thinnest, strongest material; best conductor | Electronics, sensors, composites |
| **Coke** | Amorphous carbon | Hard, porous | Metallurgy (reducing agent in iron extraction) |
| **Charcoal** | Amorphous carbon | Porous, adsorptive | Fuel, water purification (activated charcoal), gas masks |

**Diamond vs Graphite Comparison:**

| Property | Diamond | Graphite |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Structure | 3D tetrahedral (sp³) | Layered hexagonal (sp²) |
| Bonding | All 4 valence e⁻ in C-C bonds | 3 in C-C bonds; 1 delocalised |
| Hardness | Hardest known material | Soft (layers slip) |
| Conductivity | Insulator (all e⁻ in bonds) | Conductor (free e⁻) |
| Appearance | Transparent, colourless | Black/grey, opaque |
| Density | High (3.51 g/cm³) | Lower (2.26 g/cm³) |
| Use | Jewellery, cutting | Pencils, electrode, lubricant |

## 3. Organic Compounds — Homologous Series

### 3.1 Definition
A homologous series is a group of compounds with:
- Same general formula
- Same functional group
- Similar chemical properties
- Successive members differ by CH₂ (14 mass units)

### 3.2 Types of Carbon Chains

| Type | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Straight chain | All carbons in a row | n-butane |
| Branched chain | Side chains present | Isobutane |
| Cyclic | Closed ring | Cyclohexane, benzene |

### 3.3 Major Homologous Series

**Alkanes (saturated hydrocarbons):**
```
General formula: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
Bonds: all C-C single bonds
Naming: -ane suffix
```
| n | Name | Formula | State | Boiling Point |
|---|------|---------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Methane | CH₄ | Gas | −161.5°C |
| 2 | Ethane | C₂H₆ | Gas | −88.6°C |
| 3 | Propane | C₃H₈ | Gas | −42°C |
| 4 | Butane | C₄H₁₀ | Gas | −0.5°C |
| 5 | Pentane | C₅H₁₂ | Liquid | 36°C |
| 6 | Hexane | C₆H₁₄ | Liquid | 69°C |

**Alkenes (unsaturated, one double bond):**
```
General formula: CₙH₂ₙ
Contains: one C=C double bond
Naming: -ene suffix
Example: Ethylene (C₂H₄), Propylene (C₃H₆)
```

**Alkynes (unsaturated, one triple bond):**
```
General formula: CₙH₂ₙ₋₂
Contains: one C≡C triple bond
Naming: -yne suffix
Example: Acetylene/Ethyne (C₂H₂), Propyne (C₃H₄)
```

## 6. LPG, CNG, and Natural Gas

| Gas | Full Form | Composition | Use |
|-----|----------|------------|-----|
| LPG | Liquefied Petroleum Gas | Propane (C₃H₈) + Butane (C₄H₁₀) | Cooking gas, heating |
| CNG | Compressed Natural Gas | Mostly Methane (CH₄) | Vehicles, cooking |
| Natural gas | — | Mostly Methane (CH₄) | Fuel, power plants |
| Biogas | — | CH₄ (50–65%) + CO₂ | Rural fuel, cooking |

> **LPG smell:** LPG itself is odourless — **ethyl mercaptan (C₂H₅SH)** is added as odorant for leakage detection.

## 7. Fermentation and Distillation

**Fermentation:**
```
C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂
 Glucose   Yeast   Ethanol   Carbon dioxide
```
- Yeast contains enzyme **zymase**
- Temperature: 25–35°C
- **Ethanol produced:** ~5–15% concentration maximum by fermentation

**Distillation:**
- Ethanol boils at **78.4°C**, water at 100°C → distillation separates them
- Industrial alcohol: ~95.5% ethanol + 4.5% water (azeotrope — cannot be further concentrated by distillation)
- Absolute alcohol: 100% ethanol (dried with CaO)

## 8. Urea — Historical Significance

- **Urea was the first organic compound synthesised from inorganic materials (1828)**
- Synthesised by **Friedrich Wöhler** from ammonium cyanate
- Disproved the "vital force" theory (which said organic compounds could only be made by living organisms)
- This event marked the birth of modern organic chemistry

## 9. Important Reaction Types in Organic Chemistry

| Reaction | Definition | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Combustion | Organic compound + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O | CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O |
| Substitution | H replaced by another atom/group | CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl (in sunlight) |
| Addition | Atoms add across double/triple bond | C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆ (hydrogenation) |
| Elimination | Removal of atoms to form double bond | C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O (dehydration) |
| Condensation | Two molecules combine, small molecule expelled | Amino acids → proteins + H₂O |
| Saponification | Ester + base → alcohol + soap | Fat + NaOH → glycerol + soap |

## 10. Exam Traps

> ⚠️ **TRAP 1:** **Methanol is EXTREMELY POISONOUS** — as little as 10 mL can cause blindness; 30 mL can be fatal. Ethanol is the "drinking alcohol." Do not confuse them.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 2:** **Diamond and Graphite are both pure carbon** — allotropes, completely different properties due to bonding structure.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 3:** **Urea was the FIRST organic compound synthesised** (Wöhler, 1828) — historical milestone disproved vital force theory.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 4:** **Acetylene (C₂H₂)** is used in welding — NOT methane or propane (which are used in cooking/fuel).

> ⚠️ **TRAP 5:** **LPG has odour artificially added** (ethyl mercaptan) — pure LPG is odourless.

> ⚠️ **TRAP 6:** **Glucose and fructose are isomers** (same formula C₆H₁₂O₆, different structures) — glucose = grape sugar; fructose = fruit sugar (sweeter).

> ⚠️ **TRAP 7:** **Graphite is a NON-METAL** but conducts electricity. Diamond is a NON-METAL and does NOT conduct electricity.
